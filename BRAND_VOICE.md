# Brand Voice: brag.fast

**Personality in one line:** A cheeky breakfast diner for developers who ship fast but hate making social graphics.

## Core Metaphor: Breakfast/Restaurant

Everything maps to cooking:
- **Templates** = recipes
- **Generating images** = cooking ("cooking... 42%")
- **Dashboard** = kitchen
- **Pricing** = menu board
- **Customization** = seasoning
- **Delivering images** = plating
- **Support** = "Talk to the chef"

## Personality Traits

- **Playful, approachable, developer-friendly, slightly cheeky**
- Warm, welcoming (breakfast diner energy)
- Fast-paced and direct (no corporate speak)
- Indie and scrappy (relatable to founders/hackers)
- Retro/nostalgic (pixel art, NES-style UI buttons and cards)

## Tone Spectrum

| Dimension | Where brag.fast sits |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Formal <-> Casual | **80% casual** -- cheeky, never corporate |
| Serious <-> Playful | **75% playful** -- puns welcome, but not clown energy |
| Technical <-> Simple | **60% technical** -- curl examples in the hero, but accessible |
| Verbose <-> Terse | **80% terse** -- short punchy lines, no fluff |

## Voice Rules

1. **Speak like a diner cook, not a SaaS marketer.** "Show what you've been cooking" not "Streamline your content pipeline"
2. **Developer-native.** Curl examples > marketing screenshots. API-first framing.
3. **Short sentences. Punchy.** "Ship a release, we plate it." Not paragraphs.
4. **Retro-playful visual language.** NES arrows (&#9656;), pixel fonts, gold/charcoal/cream palette
5. **Remove friction verbally.** "No credit card required" repeated 5+ times. "Zero design tools." "Hands-free."
6. **Food puns: yes. Forced puns: no.** The metaphor should feel natural, not labored.

## Emotional Territory

- **The problem we name:** "Announcing launches is a chore. You skip social images because design is annoying."
- **The feeling we sell:** Effortless bragging. Ship -> post -> done.
- **Who we talk to:** Indie hackers and solopreneurs who ship frequently but hate opening Canva.

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Headlines | Punchy metaphor | "Show what you've been cooking" |
| Features | Metaphor + benefit | "Season everything to taste" |
| CTAs | Direct, low-friction | "Get 10 Free Credits" |
| Onboarding | Warm, encouraging | "Here's how to get cooking:" |
| Empty states | Encouraging nudge | "Time to cook!" |
| Errors | Neutral, minimal | "Something went wrong" -> "Try Again" |
| Support | Warm, personal | "Talk to the chef" |
| Loading | Whimsical | "cooking... 67%" |
