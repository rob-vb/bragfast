<objective>
Extend render-core's LocalVideoRenderRequest with a typed onProgress callback and wire it into the renderMedia call. This is the one render-core API change the entire Phase 6 depends on (D-10). Without onProgress, frame progress cannot reach the terminal or Workspace.

Purpose: Enable the CLI to stream frame-render progress from Remotion's renderMedia to the job store and terminal, satisfying RND-02 success criterion 3.
Output: Updated types.ts and video.ts; unit tests confirming the callback fires with correct renderedFrames and totalFrames.
</objective>
